About

His story

Pusheeno is a dinosaur-cat: part cat, part dinosaur, entirely himself. He hatched on April 9, 2017 and lives in Portland, Oregon, in between trips.

He is fierce. He wears a permanent resting grumpy face. And underneath it all, he is the most loyal plushie you will ever meet.

He lives to eat and to explore. He has stood on Antarctic ice, discovered pandan in Singapore, and never says no to a matcha. Along the way he has collected a lot of friends, and when he isn't travelling he is on emotional support duty, a job he takes very seriously.
